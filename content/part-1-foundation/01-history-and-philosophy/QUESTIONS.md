# Chapter 01 — Questions

Recall questions. Answer before unfolding; re-read [`README.md`](./README.md) if stuck.

<details>
<summary>1. What three problems at Google was Go designed to solve?</summary>

Slow builds, unmanageable dependencies, and the difficulty of writing concurrent
programs at scale — plus the general complexity of C++/Java for large engineering
teams. Go optimizes for readability and fast compilation over language power.

</details>

<details>
<summary>2. Summarize the Go philosophy in one sentence. How does it differ from the JS/TS mindset?</summary>

"Make it obvious/boring, not clever." Go favours radical simplicity and one clear
way to do things, where JS/TS culture often rewards elegant/clever abstractions. In
Go, being clever usually means you're doing it wrong.

</details>
